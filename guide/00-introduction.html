00 · Introduction ​

AceCrush Craft — a tennis-equipment data tool. Offline-first, focused on calculation and tracking.

What problem does it solve? ​

After a few years of playing, the bag holds several rackets, a few different tension settings, and you can't remember "when did I last restring". Racket specs, overgrip compensation, tension conversion, AI grip measurement — these are all "tooling work" that no one wants to do with ten different webpages or a sprawling Excel file.

AceCrush Craft is one small App with four features that cover the day-to-day:

#FeatureWhat it does
1AI grip sizeTake a photo of your palm → recommended grip size
2Racket profileRecord each racket's specs & notes, all on-device
3Stringing logOne line per restring + reminders
4Tension conversionDT / RT / lbs / kg cross-calculator → sends the result straight into the stringing log

All data is local SQLite, no upload.

Three pillars ​

1. Offline-first ​

Apart from these, everything works offline:

  • Feedback submission (only sent when you press "Send")

Racket profile, stringing log, grip-measurement results, reminder schedule — all in the device's local SQLite; uninstalling wipes them; nothing is uploaded to any server.

2. Non-intrusive ​

  • No push matrix: notifications fire only for "time to restring" alerts
  • No user-account system: V1.0 is fully no-login

3. Independently verifiable ​

  • Each calculator's input & formula is spelled out in the relevant chapter
  • The AI grip measurement error budget is given in 02 · AI grip
  • All network activity is itemized in 06 · Privacy & permissions

What V1.0 is not doing ​

To stay focused, these are out of scope for V1.0:

  • ❌ Live video / match recording / community
  • ❌ Coaching booking / instructor matchmaking / training camps
  • ❌ In-app store / e-commerce
  • ❌ Cross-device sync (V1.0 has no cloud backup)
  • ❌ Smartwatch / band integrations
  • ❌ Friends / follows / comments
